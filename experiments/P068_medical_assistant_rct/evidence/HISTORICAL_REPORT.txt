## Original source problems preserved, not repaired as success criteria

1. **The original underestimation test compares answer_delta against gold urgency.** The code forms response-minus-gold and then compares this error to the gold urgency itself. These are different quantities; its extremely small p-values do not test the stated response-versus-gold hypothesis. The original formula and all four original U values are reproduced exactly as fixed gates. A preregistered, separately labeled diagnostic compares response urgency to gold urgency and is independently audited; it also finds lower response acuity in every arm, but is not substituted into the original gates. Both tests are unpaired original-style response-level comparisons.

| Arm | Underestimate | Correct | Overestimate | Original delta-versus-gold p | Diagnostic response-versus-gold p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GPT-4o | 217 | 262 | 121 | 9.01e-142 | 0.00022 |
| Llama 3 | 219 | 265 | 116 | 1.72e-127 | 5.09e-05 |
| Command R+ | 257 | 250 | 93 | 1.04e-142 | 1.49e-10 |
| Control | 245 | 254 | 101 | 7.59e-133 | 2.65e-07 |

2. **The CommandR+ effect-size orientation is reversed.** The original printout uses Control versus CommandR+, reporting f=.514368, whereas GPT-4o and Llama3 use model versus Control. In consistent model-versus-Control orientation CommandR+ is .485632. Its p=.366053 is unchanged. The original orientation and claim are retained, and the inversion is disclosed rather than silently harmonized.

3. **Repeated responses are treated as independent in the original analysis.** Up to two scenarios come from the same participant; all original response-level tests and intervals retain this design assumption. No fresh clustered analysis is claimed.

4. **Original protocol and implementation differ.** The archived2024 registration specifies a four-level urgency scale and300people per arm; the released trial uses five levels and1298people total. The reproduction follows the observed original publication/code with all released records, not an invented reconstruction of an earlier design. Original gold-list spellings/encoding defects and matching false-positive/negative limitations are preserved. The original200-case manual threshold calibration is not repeated.

5. **Bootstrap endpoints vary across original sources.** The stored notebook reports control .56-.68 versus manuscript .55-.67; GPT-4o .42-.55 versus .42-.54; Llama3 .39-.49 versus .39-.50. All endpoint references were reviewed and retained before outcomes. Their differences are explicit in the preregistered targets.
