## What was reproduced

All 91 records from both campuses, nine criteria plus totals, three rater pairs and single/average ICCs were retained: 60 Table 5 entries, 20 Table 2 duplicates and six Abstract values. The common-rater cohort is N=90 for criterion 6 and N=91 elsewhere.

## Agreement and differences

All 86 ICC targets are within 0.010; maximum error is 0.005791159. All 20 ordering claims, 135 integrity checks per route and 42,785 independent comparisons pass. Human–human agreement remains higher than human–AI agreement at the tested endpoints. Five strict failed targets remain.
