## Original inputs and method

The 13 effect sizes and standard errors are transcribed from the visually inspected original Figure 2 on publisher PDF page 9, archived locally as paper_preprint_v1.pdf and forest_page_9.png. The arXiv upload reproduces the publisher PDF and has the original DOI and publisher footer. original_data.csv preserves the displayed decimal strings and model subgroup assignments. Figure 2 explicitly says inverse variance, random effects; the Methods identify Review Manager 5.4. The publicly archived Cochrane *Statistical algorithms in Review Manager 5* defines the DerSimonian-Laird estimator, normal intervals, heterogeneity, and random-effects subgroup comparisons. Use those original algorithms, not current RevMan Web's newer estimators.

For each set of studies use fixed inverse-variance weights 1/SE_i^2 to estimate Q and the DerSimonian-Laird between-study variance; truncate that variance at zero. Use random weights 1/(SE_i^2+tau^2) to pool the effect and calculate its standard error. Confidence intervals are normal 95% intervals. Z tests are two-sided. I-squared is max((Q-df)/Q,0) times 100. Fit the overall sample and each subgroup separately. Test subgroup differences using the inverse variances of the two random-effects subgroup estimates and the chi-square test with one degree of freedom, following the original RevMan 5 algorithm. Displayed individual and subgroup weights use the overall fit's random-effects weights. Also compute the original paper's descriptive probability-of-superiority transformation Phi(SMD/sqrt(2)).
